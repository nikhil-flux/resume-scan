Questions

What is an ATS?

An applicant tracking system collects and filters applications. It reads your CV into fields like name, jobs and skills, so messy formatting can hide your details.

Does a high score guarantee interviews?

No. The score shows how readable and strong your CV is. Fit, timing and the employer's own process still matter.

Is my CV saved anywhere?

No. The analysis runs entirely in your browser and your text is never sent to a server.

Why does my PDF show no text?

It's probably an image or scan. An ATS can't read it either, so re-export it from your editor with selectable text.

How the check works

Parse test

We look for contact details, standard section headings, and symbols that confuse ATS parsers.

Quality test

We check length, action verbs, measurable results, and weak phrases that dilute your impact.

Keyword match

Add a job description and see which of its important words your CV already contains.

ResumeScan gives guidance, not a guarantee. Every ATS works a little differently, so use the score as a checklist for improvement.
